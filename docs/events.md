# Events

`/events` opens a responsive photo archive: three columns on desktop, two on tablets and one on phones. Cards show the workshop, title, photo count and date, with a staggered entrance, a raised hover state and a gallery link. A railway diagram sits behind the archive. Twin rails and sleepers run between desktop columns; a thin vertical railway runs along the right edge on phones, in its own gutter beside the cards. Railcarts follow the document's scroll position directly from Lenis, with native scroll updates on touch devices and for reduced motion. The decorative track clips its moving layer so it cannot enlarge the document's scroll range.

Both ride bars open the same invitation. Accepting starts a green flash and tunnel transition while the lazy-loaded ride renders underneath. The transition waits for the first rendered scene (or the accessible fallback) before fading away. Returning restores focus to the originating bar. Query parameters cannot bypass the invitation.

`src/events/stations.ts` maps stations 1–8 to inauguration, dev, khoj, linkedin, n8n, noesis, unlocked and coding. The first seven retain their fixed platforms; Coding gets another safe stop. `workshop-content.ts` discovers assets under `workshops/`; `photo-order.ts` orders both numbered filenames and names such as `photo(1)`. Dates and recaps that have not been supplied retain their placeholders.

The shared `Book` serves both the archive and the ride. Desktop opens with the story on the left and photograph 1 on the right, followed by pairs of photographs. On phones (including touch landscape), the first page contains only the full-width, scrollable event story. Turn once for photograph 1, again for photograph 2, and continue through every photo on its own page. The mobile Fit control toggles between filling the page and showing the complete, uncropped photo, including photograph 1. Desktop images preserve the full frame with `object-fit: contain`. A final unpaired desktop image faces blank paper. The mobile dialog uses the dynamic viewport and safe-area insets, with compact controls below the photo.

The local Lenis instance drives the page angle, footer railcart and settling motion. Wheel input uses time-corrected exponential interpolation. After 140ms without input, a cubic curve preserves the incoming speed and settles to rest in the current direction; it never rounds back across a completed leaf. This avoids a delayed kick in speed when a small wheel notch commits a full turn. Reversals take over at the visible position. Touch capture starts on pointer-down, before a turn can replace the touched content. Both the wrapper and nested story explicitly reserve single-finger gestures, preventing the browser from cancelling a swipe halfway through. Reading swipes stay with the story through its edge and coast briefly after release; a new swipe at the edge turns the page. The story uses larger mobile type and spacing, and retains its reading position on return and on the turning leaf. Photo taps still open the original image.

Connected strips bend horizontally on desktop and vertically on phones. Their transforms and shade opacities update directly on composited layers; the leaf angle does not inherit into its content. Animation values no longer cascade through every duplicate photo and text node each frame. React commits only at leaf/phase boundaries, synchronously before painting the angle, and reuses the leaf content when a turn starts or ends. Leaves and images are keyed to their content so a completed leaf's composited photo cannot be reused for the next turn. Scroll anchoring is disabled on the page-turn wrapper. Adjacent images are decoded in both directions; the two faces have a small depth separation. Layout changes restore the remapped photograph before native scroll events can publish an old position. The document Lenis instance is disabled while an archive book or invitation is open. Arrow keys, buttons, Escape, focus restoration and background scroll locking remain available. One extra turn closes the book.

Arrival time scaling bottoms out at 72% rather than 22%, while the braking solver still docks exactly. The arrival caption remains centered instead of drifting out of the camera view. Once parked, the camera pans right over 360ms of real elapsed time and opens the book immediately; the caption fades during the pan. Depth of field stays disabled through docking and the open book.

Touch devices and hardware with limited cores or memory start with the smaller effects budget and can recover up to medium quality, keeping the expensive depth effect disabled. Screen resolution is managed separately so reducing effects preserves sharp edges.

Validation: `npm run build`, `npm test`, and Playwright coverage in `events-page.spec.mjs`, `events-railway.spec.mjs`, `book-scroll.spec.mjs`, `book-gestures.spec.mjs`, `book-motion.spec.mjs`, `book-smoothness.spec.mjs`, and `ride-book.spec.mjs`. The focused tests inspect both swipe directions, short-screen story scrolling, photo taps, wheel boundary settlement and reversals, full-screen mobile photo panels, Fit mode, viewport changes, railcart synchronization and frame-by-frame photo/angle consistency under sixfold CPU throttling. The smoothness regression samples the rendered cart transform and checks that settlement has no abrupt second jump in speed.
